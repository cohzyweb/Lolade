DESIGNING EXPERIENCES THAT CONNECT PEOPLE AND PRODUCTS

I design digital experiences that help people solve everyday problems. I care about making tools feel intuitive, honest, and useful.

I've worked closely with product managers, engineers and business teams, taking on different levels of responsibility across product design, collaboration and design leadership. The work has ranged from improving existing products to shaping new ideas and figuring out how they should work in the real world.

I'm also the founder of Forma, an AI-assisted product for designers. I conceived, designed and built it independently, taking it from an idea to a working product. Building Forma has given me the opportunity to work through the product decisions, technical considerations and business questions that come with being responsible for an entire product, not just its design.

My first degree was in Production Engineering at the University of Benin. That background still influences how I approach design. I'm naturally interested in how things work, how different parts of a system affect one another and how to make complicated processes work better.

Some of my most meaningful work happens outside my day-to-day product design role.

I tutor Product Design at TS Academy, where I help aspiring designers understand the discipline, work through practical projects and develop the confidence to make their own design decisions. I enjoy teaching because it gives me a reason to keep questioning what I know and find better ways to explain things people who use it.

I'm also involved in the wider design and technology community. Through Women Techmakers Lagos' BuildHer programme, I've supported women growing their skills and careers in tech. I serve as Technical Director at DigiHive Network, where I've contributed to building a community around learning, mentorship and career growth. I'm also part of Oguntechies, a community supporting young creatives in Ogun State.

I've also spoken at events including Google DevFest Lagos, where I've shared ideas around gamification in UX and designing for trust in an AI-driven world. I enjoy conversations that challenge how we think about technology and the people who use it.

Outside all of these, you’ll probably find me nose-deep in a romance or thriller novel, binge watching k-drama, or engaging in behind-the-scenes stories on LinkedIn and Twitter. I love conversations that blend tech, culture, and ethics, and I’m always curious about how we build better, more honest digital experiences.
