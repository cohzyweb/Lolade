ABOUT ME

Hi, my name is Ololade, and I am a Product Designer who enjoys solving real problems through thoughtful, human-centred design.

Since 2021, I've designed digital products across SaaS, fintech, edtech, logistics and developer tools, working with teams across the world. Along the way, I've worked with Ajua, Tappi, Geny Labs and JBits Technology.

I also built Forma independently, taking an idea from concept to a working AI-assisted product.
